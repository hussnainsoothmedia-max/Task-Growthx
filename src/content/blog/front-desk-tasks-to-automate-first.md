The best first automation is not the most impressive one. It's the task your team does many times a day, the same way every time, where a mistake is easy to spot and fix. Here are five that fit that description in most dental practices.

## 1. Confirming appointments

Calling patients the day before is pure routine. An automated reminder with "confirm, reschedule or cancel" options does the same job, and reception only calls the patients who don't reply.

## 2. Collecting booking requests from every channel

Requests arrive through the website, WhatsApp, Google and email. Pulling them into one place, in one format, saves the retyping and makes sure nothing sits unread in an inbox nobody checked.

## 3. Sending forms before the first visit

New patients need a medical history form and consent. Sending the link automatically when the appointment is booked, plus one reminder if it isn't filled in, means fewer clipboards at the desk on the day.

## 4. Recall messages

Patients due for a check-up or cleaning can be found in the practice software and messaged each week with a booking link. Without automation, the recall list tends to wait until someone has a quiet afternoon.

## 5. Asking for reviews

A short message after the visit asking how it went, with a review link for happy patients, is easy to forget by hand. Automated, it goes out at the right moment every time, and any unhappy reply reaches the practice manager privately first.

## How to choose between them

Ask your reception team which of these takes the most of their day. Start there, run it for a few weeks, and only then add the next one. One workflow your team trusts is worth more than five they work around.
